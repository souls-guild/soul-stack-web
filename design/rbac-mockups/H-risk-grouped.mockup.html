Risk groups (Read / Write / Destructive) with group-level "All" let you grant "read-only" in one click and immediately surface dangerous permissions — a colored bar and "⚠ High-impact" highlight destroy / view-secrets. Downside: a risk-based layout hides the familiar alphabetical order of actions within a resource.
