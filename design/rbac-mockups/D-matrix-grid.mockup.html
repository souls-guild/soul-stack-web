The matrix shows the whole catalog at once: where a permission is granted (✓), where it isn't yet, and where an action doesn't apply (·). "All" per row = resource.*; "All" per column grants a verb across every resource at once — minimal clicks. The flip side: with many actions the columns grow wide, some cells are always inapplicable (gaps), and precise scope shows only when a cell is selected.
